# Part 2 Finalization — Solution Rationale, AI-Native MVP, User Testing & Metrics

## 1. Problem Summary (From Discovery Engine & 6 User Interviews)

- **Chosen Target Segment:** **Young Salaried First-Time SIP Investors (Age 22–28, ₹1,000–₹5,000/month SIP)** in Months 2–9 of their first mutual fund SIP (38% of our 241 analysed reviews).
- **How a SIP Gets Cancelled on Groww ([Official Groww Help Rule](https://groww.in/help/mutual-funds/order/why-was-my-order-cancelled--68)):**
  1. **Voluntary Cancellation Request:** User taps `Cancel SIP` in the app.
  2. **Automatic Cancellation (3-Strike Rule):** 3 consecutive monthly instalments go unpaid (low balance / failed autopay).
- **Why Good-Intent Beginners Drop Off ([Myfolios Behavioural Breakdown](https://www.myfolios.in/blog/why-people-stop-their-sip) + 241 Reviews + 6 Interviews):**
  - **Reason 1: "Money got tight this month"** — Salary delayed to the 8th, or medical/festival expenses hit. Because Groww does not let users lower an existing SIP amount in-place (must cancel and recreate) and blocks skipping inside 2–3 days of debit, users fear **₹295–₹590 bank NACH bounce fees** and cancel the entire SIP.
  - **Reason 2: "The market fell & my SIP is in loss"** — Seeing `-2.42%` 1Y return on *SBI Nifty Next 50 Index Fund* (or `-18% XIRR` on a ₹180 dip) makes beginners think they are losing money, missing that lower NAV (`₹18.71`) buys more units.
  - **Reason 3: "It feels too slow / barely moving"** — In Year 1, a ₹2,000 SIP shows tiny rupee gains (`₹140` in 4 months) with no named goal attached.
  - **Reason 4: "A friend's fund did better"** — Seeing `+18.46%` 5Y returns on another fund tempts users to stop and switch early, getting hit by **1% exit load (<12 months) and 20% STCG tax**.
  - **Reason 5: "Autopay failed once and never retried"** — When a debit fails on the due date, there is no 1-tap UPI retry, pushing passive users toward the 3-strike auto-cancellation.

## 2. Solution Rationale: Groww "Smart SIP Shield" (Inside the Groww App)

### What It Is
An **eval-gated AI feature embedded inside Groww’s `Mutual Funds → SIPs → Manage / Cancel SIP` mobile flow** (matching the *SBI Nifty Next 50 Index Fund Direct Growth* screens in `photos/`).

When a user taps **`Cancel / Pause SIP`** or receives a **`Missed SIP Instalment`** alert, instead of a plain *"Are you sure you want to cancel?"* box, **Smart SIP Shield** asks what is on their mind (via the 4 quick reasons or free-text/Hinglish input) and generates a personalized, math-backed retention option.

### Where AI Genuinely Earns Its Place (vs. What a Non-AI Version Misses)

| Dimension | What a Static Non-AI Version Does (And Misses) | Where AI Genuinely Earns Its Place |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **1. Multi-Factor User Intent (English + Hinglish)** | Forces the user to click a single static button (`Pause` or `Cancel`). Fails when a user has a combined doubt: *"Salary is late till 9th and my SBI Nifty Next 50 is -2.4% red—should I stop this ₹2,000 SIP or move to Small Cap?"* | Parses compound free-text/Hinglish intent—separating the **cash-flow fix** (step down to ₹500 min SIP or move date from `1st → 9th`) from the **market-dip anxiety** while **blocking the fund-switching advice request**. |
| **2. Personalized Folio-Math Translation** | Shows generic static FAQs (*"Rupee cost averaging is good"*). Users ignore generic text when stressed. | Computes and explains the user's **own exact SIP numbers** in plain words: e.g., *"At ₹18.71 NAV, your ₹2,000 SIP bought **106.89 units** (+11.4 extra units compared to last month's higher NAV)"* or *"Redeeming now costs **1% exit load + 20% STCG tax**; lowering to ₹500 (Min SIP) for 1 month costs **₹0**."* |
| **3. Real-Time Safety & Compliance Gate** | Cannot dynamically detect if a user is asking for stock/fund tips or reacting to finfluencer hype. | Runs an **Eval Gate** on every input/output: automatically triggers a **Safe Fallback** whenever a user asks *"Which fund should I buy?"* or *"Will the market fall further?"* |

## 3. Eval-Gated AI Architecture & Safe Fallback Design

Every response generated by **Smart SIP Shield** is gated by a **4-Check Real-Time Evaluation Pipeline** visible in the UI:

1. **Check 1 — No Investment Advice Guardrail (`PASS` / `SAFE FALLBACK`):**
   - Scans both user prompt and AI output for fund recommendations, stock picks, or return predictions.
   - **Safe Fallback Triggered:** If a user asks *"Should I switch from SBI Nifty Next 50 to Bandhan Small Cap?"* or *"Which fund will give 20% this year?"*, the Eval Gate blocks the LLM response and renders a deterministic **Safe Fallback Card**:
     - **Declines to answer:** Explicitly states Groww cannot recommend specific funds or predict returns.
     - **Provides factual rules only:** Shows the user's current exit-load window (1% if redeemed <12 months) and STCG tax rule (20%).
     - **Points to official sources:** Links directly to [Groww Help Center](https://groww.in/help/mutual-funds/order/why-was-my-order-cancelled--68) and [AMFI Investor Corner](https://www.amfiindia.com).
2. **Check 2 — No Fear / FOMO / Urgency Guardrail (`PASS`):**
   - Blocks any manipulative countdowns, guilt-tripping, or "you will miss out on wealth" phrasing. Ensures the **`Cancel SIP Anyway`** button is always visible.
3. **Check 3 — Folio-Math Grounding Check (`PASS`):**
   - Verifies that every rupee, unit, NAV (`₹18.71`), and minimum SIP (`₹500`) number matches the user's actual folio state with zero hallucinated return projections.
4. **Golden Set Quality Benchmark (Measured Against Our 20-Item Evidence Log Golden Set):**
   - All 20 hand-labelled Golden Set scenarios from our AI Evidence Log are tested against the MVP's Eval Gate:
     - **Root-Cause Intent Diagnosis:** `95% (19/20)`
     - **No Fund/Stock Advice Compliance:** `100% (20/20)`
     - **No Fear/FOMO Compliance:** `100% (20/20)`
     - **Safe Fallback Trigger on Out-of-Scope / F&O Noise (`EV-015`):** `100% (Pass)`

## 4. Core User Flows in the MVP (Based on Your 3 Groww Screenshots)

Using the **SBI Nifty Next 50 Index Fund Direct Growth** (`Min. SIP ₹500`, `1Y Return -2.42%`, `NAV ₹18.71`, `Monthly on 1st`):

1. **Flow A — "Money Got Tight This Month" (Cash-Flow Shield):**
   - **Action Offered:**
     - **Option 1:** *Lower next month's SIP from ₹2,000 → **₹500 (Min. SIP)** for 1 month (auto-reverts to ₹2,000 next month).*
     - **Option 2:** *Pause SIP for 1–2 months with a guaranteed **Auto-Restart Date**.*
     - **Option 3:** *Change SIP instalment date from **1st → 7th** (after salary credit) using Groww's calendar picker to avoid ₹295 bank NACH bounce fees.*
2. **Flow B — "The Market Fell (`-2.42%` 1Y) & My SIP Is in Loss":**
   - **Action Offered:** Shows the **Unit Accumulation Card** (*"Your ₹2,000 at ₹18.71 NAV bought **106.89 units**—buying more units while NAV is lower"*), with options to **[Keep SIP Running]** or **[Step Down to ₹500 for 1 Month]**.
3. **Flow C — "It Feels Too Slow in Year 1":**
   - **Action Offered:** Shows **5/12 Months Habit Progress Bar** + 1-tap **Goal Tagging** (*Emergency Fund*, *Bike/Travel*, *Long-Term Wealth*).
4. **Flow D — "Autopay Failed on SIP Day (Stop 3-Strike Auto-Cancel)":**
   - **Action Offered:** Shows Groww's official rule (*"SIPs auto-cancel after 3 consecutive unpaid instalments — currently 1/3 missed"*) + **[Pay ₹2,000 via UPI in 1 Tap]** or **[Skip This Month Safely]**.
5. **Flow E — "A Friend's Fund Did Better / Which Fund Should I Buy?" (Eval-Gate Safe Fallback):**
   - **Action Offered:** Triggers **Safe Fallback**—declines fund advice, displays exact exit-load/STCG tax impact of redeeming early, and links to official AMFI/SEBI guides.

## 6. Metrics Framework (Business Metric, Leading Indicators, Guardrails & AI Quality Eval)

| Category | Metric Name | Exact Definition | Rationale for Choosing |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Business Metric (North Star)** | **12-Month Active SIP Continuation Rate** | `% of first-time MF investors with ≥1 successful SIP instalment in Month 12 (last 30 days) ÷ total first-time SIP starters 12 months prior` | Directly measures long-term habit survival and compounding user/platform value. |
| **Leading Indicator 1** | **Cancel-Screen Save Rate** | `% of users tapping "Cancel SIP" who choose Pause (with auto-restart) or Step-Down to ₹500 instead of full cancellation` | Immediate signal of whether the Smart SIP Shield prevents voluntary churn at the moment of doubt. |
| **Leading Indicator 2** | **Failed-Debit 72h UPI Recovery Rate** | `% of failed monthly auto-debits completed via the 1-Tap UPI Retry link within 72 hours` | Directly stops involuntary churn before 3 consecutive misses trigger Groww's automatic cancellation. |
| **Leading Indicator 3** | **Scheduled Auto-Resume Success Rate** | `% of paused or stepped-down SIPs that successfully debit on their scheduled auto-resume date` | Confirms that pausing/stepping down actually preserves 12-month retention rather than merely delaying drop-off. |
| **Guardrail Metric 1** | **Average Monthly SIP Contribution per Cohort User** | `Total monthly ₹ invested ÷ total active first-year cohort users` | Ensures the "Lower to ₹500" option doesn't cannibalize healthy ₹2,000–₹5,000 SIPs among users who aren't cash-strapped. |
| **Guardrail Metric 2** | **NACH Bounce / Cutoff Complaint Rate** | `% of Pause/Skip users who still experience a bank debit or bounce fee due to T-2 clearing lock-in` | Protects trust—ensures the UI only offers instant Skip/Pause when outside the bank's irreversible presentation window. |
| **AI Quality Metric 1** | **Eval-Gate Safety Pass Rate** | `% of AI responses passing "No Investment Advice" and "No Fear/FOMO" checks (Target: 100% via deterministic Safe Fallback)` | Regulatory and assignment constraint; zero tolerance for fund tips or urgency nudges. |
| **AI Quality Metric 2** | **Golden Set Diagnosis & Math Accuracy** | `% agreement between the MVP's root-cause diagnosis & folio math vs. our 20-item hand-labelled Golden Set` | Ensures the AI reliably distinguishes cash crunches, market dips, expectation gaps, and out-of-scope F&O/advice queries. |
